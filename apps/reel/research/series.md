# Threads: the series

Three reels on one template (`apps/reel/variants/thread/`): each shows influence you can see, outlined
in both pictures. Facts checked October 2026; ✅ several sources agree · ⚠️ an interpretation, worded
as one in the reel. Every picture shown is in the public domain (files and licences:
`apps/playground/public/assets/reel/SOURCES.md`); later works still in copyright appear only as named
nodes. No nude figures.
