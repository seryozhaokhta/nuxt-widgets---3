# Threads: *Las Meninas* and the pictures that answer it

Research for the reel “Meninas” (variant E). Every step is a work you can **see** borrowing from the one
before: the same mirror, the same painter at the easel, the same gaze. Checked October 2026; sources under
the table. ✅ several independent sources agree · ⚠️ likely or disputed, worded carefully in the reel.
No nude figures in any of the works.

## The thread

| Year | Place | Work | What it takes, and what kind of influence | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1434 | Bruges | Jan van Eyck, *The Arnolfini Portrait* | **Motif.** A mirror at the back shows two people standing where we stand. The panel hung in the Spanish royal collection in Madrid while Velázquez was court painter and keeper of the king’s pictures; that he knew it is very likely, not documented | ⚠️ |
| 1656 | Madrid | Velázquez, *Las Meninas* | The infanta Margarita with her maids; the painter at a huge canvas we see from the back; the king and queen only in the mirror; José Nieto in the bright doorway, where the perspective meets. The red cross on Velázquez’s chest was added after 1659, when he was made a knight of Santiago — legend says the king painted it | ✅ |
| 1778 | Madrid | Goya etches a copy of *Las Meninas* | **Study.** One of his prints after Velázquez’s paintings | ⚠️ (dates vary) |
| 1800 | Madrid | Goya, *The Family of Charles IV* | **Quotation.** Goya puts himself at an easel on the left, the canvas turned away at the same angle; the royals pose in front of him — no mirror now, they face us directly | ✅ |
| 1879 → 1882 | Madrid → Paris | Sargent copies *Las Meninas* in the Prado (in the copyists’ register, 14 Oct–22 Nov 1879), then paints *The Daughters of Edward Darley Boit* | **Study → reworking.** A dark, deep room, a muted palette, a girl who looks straight at us; Henry James saw the debt at once | ✅ |
| 1957 | Cannes | Picasso, *Las Meninas* — 58 canvases (Aug–Dec), given to the Museu Picasso, Barcelona, 1968 | **Variation.** 45 of them rework the picture | ✅ |
| 1958 | | Dalí, *Velázquez Painting the Infanta Margarita with the Lights and Shadows of His Own Glory* | **Homage** | ✅ |
| 1966 | Paris | Michel Foucault, *The Order of Things* | **Theory.** The book opens with a chapter on *Las Meninas*: everyone looks at a place outside the frame — ours, and the king’s | ✅ |
| 1973 | | Richard Hamilton, *Picasso’s Meninas* (etching, for Picasso’s 90th-birthday portfolio) | **A reference to a reference.** Velázquez’s layout, each figure in a different Picasso style, Picasso at the easel; printed by Picasso’s own printer, Aldo Crommelynck | ✅ |
| 2005 | Madrid | Thomas Struth, *Museo del Prado* | **Looking at looking.** Photographs of visitors crowded in front of *Las Meninas* | ✅ |
| 2018– | Madrid | *Meninas Madrid Gallery* | **The street.** About 80 fibreglass meninas, each repainted by a different artist, along Madrid’s streets; auctioned for charity; every year since | ✅ |

The thread starts in Bruges and keeps coming back to Madrid — to the Prado, where the picture hangs.

## The reel as made (variant E, 38 s, 100 bpm, 1080×1920, English)

Influence is shown, not told: two pictures one above the other, the borrowed part outlined in gold on
both and joined by a thread, the kind of borrowing named between them. Then every work becomes a node
on a growing graph. Timings live in `apps/reel/variants/meninas.ts`.
